1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android game Letter Trails: Word Hunt (also displayed as Letter Trails - Relaxing Word Hunt, package ID com.lettertrails.wordhunt).

Letter Trails: Word Hunt is a single-player word search puzzle game that does not require account creation, sign-in, or direct submission of personal details. However, when your device is connected to the internet, integrated third-party services—specifically Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)—automatically process certain technical, device, and advertising-related data as described below.

2. Information You Provide & Local Gameplay Storage

No User Accounts: We do not require registration or login, and we do not operate a first-party backend server. The game does not ask you for your name, physical address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

On-Device Progress Storage: To preserve your progress offline, the game stores non-personal gameplay data locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Unlocked and completed trail stops, unique completion count, and stamp badges
  • In-game Gold coin balance, hint count, daily reward dates, and ad pacing flags
  • Sound, music, haptic feedback, and color-blind accessibility settings

If you email us directly at ankitrajawat010@gmail.com for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address and message solely to respond to you.

3. Automatically Collected Information & Permissions

We do not run our own analytics or crash-tracking servers. When online, integrated third-party SDKs and font requests may automatically receive technical data, including:

  • Advertising & Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and app/SDK instance identifiers used by Google AdMob.
  • Device & Network Data: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, screen dimensions, language/locale, network connectivity, and IP address.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic area inferred from IP address by Google services (no GPS or precise location permissions are used).
  • Ad Interactions & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, clicks, rewarded video completions, load performance, and SDK crash/error diagnostics.

Android Permissions: The Android manifest declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK includes com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob) & UMP Consent

Letter Trails: Word Hunt integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve advertisements:

  • Banner Ads: Adaptive banners displayed at the bottom of the Home screen and Trail Map screen only (never during puzzle gameplay).
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads displayed at natural breaks after every 3rd uniquely completed campaign level.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional, user-initiated video ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for +1 Hint or +30s continue time. Rewards are granted only upon confirmed completion from AdMob.

Consent Management via Google UMP

We integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with regional privacy regulations (such as EEA/UK GDPR and applicable U.S. state privacy laws):

  • Where required, consent information is requested at launch before loading ads, and Google's consent prompt is displayed when needed.
  • Depending on your choice, AdMob serves either personalized ads or non-personalized ads (npa). Even for non-personalized ads, Google may use IP address and basic identifiers for contextual delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement, and invalid-traffic prevention.
  • Where applicable, you can update your ad consent settings inside the game via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices, or manage/delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

The game includes an in-game Gold Shop with consumable Gold packs designed for Google Play Billing.

  • Payment Processing: In Google Play releases, all payments (cards, UPI, Google Play balance, carrier billing) are processed securely and exclusively by Google Play.
  • No Financial Data Accessed: We never access, collect, process, or store your payment card numbers, bank account details, UPI IDs, or billing address.
  • Entitlement Handling: Upon purchase confirmation from Google Play, the app receives only transaction/SKU confirmation needed to credit your in-game Gold balance or ad-removal entitlement locally.

6. Firebase, Analytics & Other Third-Party Services

Firebase & Analytics Status: Letter Trails: Word Hunt does not use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or third-party crash/analytics SDKs.

7. Data Sharing, Security & Retention

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell personal data. Data sharing is limited to automated processing by Google AdMob/UMP (based on your consent) and Google Play for billing.
  • Security: Network requests made by integrated Google SDKs and font loading use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Local game state is stored in your device's app storage sandbox.
  • Retention & Deletion: Local game data stays on your device until you tap Settings → Reset All Stops, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Support emails are deleted upon request or once resolved.

8. Children's Privacy

Letter Trails: Word Hunt is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a child has sent personal data via email, please contact ankitrajawat010@gmail.com for immediate deletion.

9. International Processing & User Privacy Rights

Google AdMob, Google UMP, and Google Play operate globally and may process technical/advertising data in countries outside your residence (including the United States).

Depending on your local laws (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK, LGPD in Brazil, or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may update ad consent choices in-app via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (when available), reset/opt out of your Advertising ID in Android Settings, or email ankitrajawat010@gmail.com regarding any support correspondence.
